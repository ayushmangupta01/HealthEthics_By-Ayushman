Why HealthEthics exists

Most fitness apps are built for someone who already has a gym membership, a dietician on speed-dial and a smartwatch. HealthEthics is built for the much larger group who has none of those — students, working professionals and families in India's Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities — and asks a simpler question: what can honest numbers and a bit of rule-based intelligence do on their own, for free, from a browser?

HealthEthics offers general wellness information and self-assessment tools. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or replace a licensed doctor, dietician or mental health professional.
